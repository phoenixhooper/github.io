CAD / SPACE PLANNING

Retail Operations Center Conversion

Supported the conversion of an existing Valero gas station into a multi-tenant retail operations center by translating client requirements into a practical interior layout.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Transform an existing gas station into a shared retail operations center capable of supporting multiple businesses within the existing building footprint.

MY ROLE

Requirements Gathering Field Measurement Space Planning Stakeholder Collaboration Technical Documentation Regulatory Compliance

PROJECT APPROACH

01

Assess

Measured and documented the existing building to establish accurate baseline conditions for the proposed conversion.

02

Analyze

Evaluated the available space and client requirements to determine how multiple businesses could operate within the existing structure.

03

Develop

Recommended the placement and sizing of an interior separation wall to create distinct business spaces without requiring exterior modifications.

04

Document

Produced properly scaled and dimensioned CAD documentation suitable for city review and project inspection requirements.

PROJECT PERSPECTIVE

Maximizing an existing space without expanding the footprint.

The challenge was to transform an existing commercial building into a space capable of supporting multiple businesses without making exterior modifications.

My role involved measuring the existing structure, understanding the client's operational requirements, and developing a practical interior solution. The proposed separation wall created distinct business areas while allowing the existing building footprint to remain unchanged.

The project demonstrates the importance of requirements analysis, stakeholder collaboration, spatial problem solving, scope awareness, and accurate documentation when translating a client's concept into an executable plan.
